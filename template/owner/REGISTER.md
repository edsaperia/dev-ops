# Dev-ops register: work left undone, and when to do it

Each entry: the item, why it waits, the condition to act (checkable without judgement: a date, a file existing, a state a command can test, a decision the owner has taken), and where. Re-read at the end of every task: condition met and trivial, do it; met and not trivial, offer it to the owner; not met, leave it.
